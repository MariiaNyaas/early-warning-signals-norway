# Methodology & QA

1. Source: SSB Bankruptcies, updated 22 Oct 2026. Periods: Q4 2025–Q3 2026 for aggregate time series; Q3 2025 vs Q3 2026 for sectors and counties.
2. National year-on-year change = (783 / 911 − 1) × 100 = −14.1%.
3. Enterprise-only by-industry totals sum to 833 in Q3 2025 and 758 in Q3 2026. County all-category totals sum to 911 / 783.
4. Sector changes use enterprise bankruptcies excluding sole proprietorships. Regional comparisons use all forms, and should not be combined with sector data without matching population.
5. Turnover is reported in thousand NOK for businesses entering bankruptcy proceedings, not loss given default or recovered amounts. Persons employed does not equal verified redundancies.
6. Suppressed data (“..”) are missing, not zero. The supplied share-capital file has a 2026 distribution that assigns all AS/ASA cases to the highest band; it is excluded pending source validation.
7. Four quarterly observations do not permit meaningful seasonality or backtesting. No bankruptcy incidence rates, PD estimates or causal claims are provided.
8. Follow-up: add active firm counts by sector, longer matched history, industry mapping controls, macroeconomic predictors, threshold calibration, rolling backtest and stability checks.
