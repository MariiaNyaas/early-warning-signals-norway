# Executive Summary | Early Warning Signals
**Norwegian Business Distress — Q3 2026**  
**Mariia Nyaas | Independent public-data research**

## Executive assessment
The number of newly opened bankruptcies fell to **783 in Q3 2026** from **911 in Q3 2025**, down **14.1% year-on-year**. This is an improvement in the aggregate count, not proof of lower default probabilities across all industries.

## Divergence across industries
Construction enterprise bankruptcies (excluding sole proprietorships) declined from **194 to 181**, whereas wholesale and retail trade increased from **144 to 148**. Other service activities increased from **14 to 27**, but the small starting base matters for interpretation.

## Decision implications
- Maintain sector-specific monitoring rather than extrapolating the national headline to all borrowers.
- Investigate trade and services dynamics with normalized rates, exposures and a sufficiently long quarterly history.
- Avoid policy changes based on raw case counts before controlling for industry population, seasonality and composition.

## Methodological status
This is a **descriptive monitoring prototype**, not a predictive early-warning model. Future validation requires indicators observed before outcomes and out-of-sample tests. No confidential bank data have been used.

**Source:** SSB, [Bankruptcies](https://www.ssb.no/en/virksomheter-foretak-og-regnskap/konkurser/statistikk/opna-konkursar), updated 22 October 2026.
